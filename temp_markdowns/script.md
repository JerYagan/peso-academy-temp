Good day. Welcome to PESO Academy — a free, web-based Learning Management System developed for the Public Employment Service Office, Special Projects Division of Quezon City. PESO Academy was designed to make quality skills training more accessible to Filipinos by providing a structured platform where learners can enroll in courses, develop new skills, track their progress, and earn certificates that recognize their achievements.

The platform addresses the need for a centralized digital training environment that serves the diverse client groups of the PESO office — from regular learners and students to returning Filipinos and persons with disabilities. By unifying training, assessment, and certification in one system, PESO Academy makes skills development more organized, measurable, and accessible.

It is worth noting that in its current deployment, PESO Academy is being used initially by PESO employees and internal staff. The system is fully designed and ready to onboard PESO clients — the broader public — in future rollouts. All the features and structures demonstrated in this walkthrough are built with that full-scale deployment in mind.

In this walkthrough, we will demonstrate the key features of the system across its three user roles: the Learner, the Trainer, and the Administrator. Each role has a dedicated workspace tailored to their specific responsibilities on the platform.

1. The PESO Academy Home Page is the public-facing entry point of the platform and can be accessed by anyone without logging in. The navigation bar at the top provides access to the Features section, which outlines the platform's core capabilities; the How It Works guide for new users; the Courses catalog; and the About page. The platform supports both English and Filipino through a language toggle in the navigation bar, and offers light and dark display modes to suit different environments. When visitors are ready to begin, they can create an account or sign in using the Login and Register button, and the system will automatically direct each user to the correct workspace based on their role.

New users register by providing their full name, email address, and a password. There are two account types available: a PESO Client account for regular learners, and a PESO Employee account for PESO office staff, which requires a valid PESO email domain and an employee ID for verification. Registration is kept brief to lower barriers to access. Once completed, the user is redirected to their learner dashboard to begin setting up their profile and exploring the platform.

On their first login, learners complete a short three-step onboarding process that personalizes their experience. In the first step, learners select their interests and preferred course categories — such as Digital Services, Entrepreneurship, Hospitality and Tourism, or Customer Service — so the system knows which training areas to prioritize for them.

The second step is a Quick Readiness Check, where learners indicate their confidence level with the selected training topics, how much time per week they can dedicate to learning, and how comfortable they are with digital tools. This data calibrates the recommendation engine so that suggested courses are appropriate for the learner's current situation and capacity. In the final step, learners confirm their suggested starting level, or select a different one manually, and declare any existing skills they already have so the engine avoids suggesting redundant content. Once all three steps are complete, the learner clicks Complete Onboarding and the platform is fully personalized for their journey.

2. After onboarding, learners arrive on their personal My Dashboard page, which provides a consolidated overview of their learning activity and performance. A Performance Summary at the top displays assessment scores and overall learning progress across all enrolled courses. The dashboard highlights the learner's last accessed course and shows the last module visited, allowing them to resume immediately where they left off. It also lists all their enrolled courses with progress bars, and features a section for personalized course recommendations based on their profile and activity history. The dashboard also includes Review which shows their profile highlights and their statistics such as total courses enrolled, completed courses, and assessment passed. The dashboard is designed to be a one-stop hub for learners to track their progress, access their courses, and discover new learning opportunities.

Through the Browse Courses page, learners can explore the full training catalog, filtering by category, skill area, or topic. Each course card shows the difficulty level, estimated duration, and a short description. Clicking on any course opens its full detail view — displaying the module structure, learning objectives, and enrollment requirements — so learners can make an informed decision before enrolling.  The Browse Courses page also features Personalized Course Recommendations powered by PESO Academy's hybrid recommendation engine, which surfaces relevant training based on the learner's profile, stated interests, and activity history.

PESO Academy includes a dedicated Progress Dashboard accessible from the top navigation bar. Unlike the main dashboard, this page is built specifically for review and reflection. It opens with a Primary Next Step card prompting the learner to resume their most recent module so they never lose their place. Below that, four summary cards display: Total Courses enrolled, Completed courses, Official Hours based on the course durations set by trainers, and the learner's Average Progress percentage across all enrollments. A Course Progress bar chart breaks down completion rates per enrolled course, while a Completion Status donut chart shows the overall distribution between courses that are still ongoing and those that have been fully completed.

Once a learner is enrolled in a course, they can access the course content through the Modules page. Each module contains a mix of text blocks, videos, documents, and quiz activities. Learners can navigate through the module sequentially or jump to specific sections using the sidebar. The system tracks their progress through each module, marking completed sections and allowing them to pick up where they left off at any time.

After the learner completes all necessary modules, they can take an assessment to test their knowledge. The assessment includes multiple-choice questions, true or false questions, and essay responses. Multiple-choice and true or false answers are automatically scored, while essay responses are saved for manual review by trainers or admins. The assessment score is calculated based on the points defined for each question in the module content, and it is stored separately from the practice quiz score. The learner's progress reflects the completion of the module and the practice quiz, but it does not affect their overall course completion status or certificate eligibility.

Once a course is completed, a certificate is automatically generated and stored on the Certifications page. Each certificate can be opened for full details, downloaded as a PDF, or printed — giving learners a verifiable and shareable record of their completed training.

The Profile page allows learners to manage their personal information and monitor their Profile Health — a completion tracker showing how many fields have been filled in. A more complete profile enables the system to generate more accurate and relevant course suggestions. Learners can also edit their profile information at any time, and the system will update their recommendations accordingly.

The Settings page gives learners control over their language preferences, including language selection, display mode, and password change.

Trainers are the course facilitators on the platform. The Trainer Dashboard opens with a Portfolio Snapshot that summarizes the total number of managed courses, the overall completion rate across their learners, and the current count of at-risk learners who may need follow-up. Priority Signals on the right highlight the most pressing areas requiring attention, and course performance metrics within the recommendation system — including impressions, click-through rates, and acceptance rates — are also visible from the dashboard.

Through the My Courses section, trainers can create new courses, modules, and assessments using an authoring interface that supports text content, videos, documents, and quiz activities. For existing courses, trainers have full editing control — updating course details, revising module content, adjusting completion requirements, temporarily unpublishing a course, or removing it entirely. Each course also features a Learner Leaderboard that ranks participants by their performance scores.

Trainers manage the platform's Taxonomy by maintaining course categories, skill tags, and topic tags that keep the catalog organized and searchable. The Verification page lets trainers review and approve learner identity submissions, and the Learners page provides a complete view of every enrolled learner, with tools to inspect progress, assessment activity, and completion status.

Trainers can also release learners certificate upon course completion. This process involves verifying that the learner has met all the necessary requirements, such as completing all modules and passing any required assessments. Once verified, trainers can manually issue the certificate to the learner, which will then be available in the learner's Certifications page for download or printing.

Administrators hold the highest level of access on PESO Academy. The Admin Dashboard opens with a Platform Snapshot covering total users, available courses, total enrollments, the platform-wide completion rate, and the count of active learners in the past thirty days. The Engagement Snapshot and Top Course Insights provide further detail on learner activity and course performance. The Predictive Risk Oversight panel identifies courses and learners flagged by the system's scoring model and generates a Learner Disengagement Watchlist, allowing administrators to intervene before learners fall too far behind.

Admin can verify learner identities through the Verification page, which shows pending submissions with the learner's name, email, and uploaded ID document. Admin can approve or reject each submission, and approved learners will receive a notification confirming their verified status.

Admin can also add, remove, and assign user roles through the User Management page, which lists all registered users with their roles, registration dates. Admin can search for specific users, filter by role or registration date, and perform bulk actions such as sending notifications or deactivating accounts.

Platform management is organized through two navigation dropdowns. The Manage dropdown includes tools for overseeing users, managing learners and assessments, courses, and taxonomy. The Insights menu contains the Reports section — the platform's analytics hub. Administrators can generate eight types of reports: Training Completion, User Activity, Certificate Issuance, Compliance, Enrollment, Rankings, Content Performance, and Staff reports. Each report is displayed as both a data table and a visual chart, and can be exported in CSV format for documentation, auditing, and program reporting purposes.

PESO Academy is a complete, role-based Learning Management System built to support skills development and continuous learning for every Filipino — for free. For learners, it delivers a personalized learning journey from onboarding to certification. For trainers, it provides full control over course creation, verification, catalog management, and learner monitoring. For administrators, it offers real-time oversight through dashboards, predictive analytics, structured management tools, and comprehensive reporting.

This concludes the walkthrough of PESO Academy — developed as a capstone project for the Public Employment Service Office, Special Projects Division of Quezon City, and built with the mission of making quality skills training accessible to every Filipino. Thank you for watching.
